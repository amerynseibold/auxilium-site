# Auxilium Business Solutions

Auxilium Business Solutions is a modern business website for a workflow automation and operations consulting brand focused on helping small and growing businesses modernize outdated systems, reduce manual admin work, and improve internal processes.

The site presents Auxilium’s services, portfolio demos, business positioning, contact flow, and supporting legal pages in a polished, responsive format.

## Live Site

[https://www.auxiliumbusiness.com](https://www.auxiliumbusiness.com)

## Overview

Auxilium Business Solutions helps businesses improve the way work moves through their organization by building practical tools, dashboards, quoting systems, and workflow automations.

This website is designed to communicate that value clearly through:

- A modern landing page
- Dedicated About, Services, Demos, and Contact pages
- Portfolio/demo project previews
- Responsive desktop and mobile layouts
- Privacy Policy and Terms pages
- A clean, professional UI built for client presentation

## Pages

- **Home** — Main positioning, hero section, featured work, process overview, audience fit, and CTA
- **About** — Background and business approach
- **Services** — Core service offerings and how Auxilium helps businesses modernize workflows
- **Demos** — Portfolio-style demo projects that show examples of quoting tools, dashboards, and workflow systems
- **Contact** — Contact form page for inquiries
- **Privacy Policy** — Basic privacy information
- **Terms** — Basic website terms

## Featured Demo Work

The site highlights example workflow solutions including:

- **SnapQuote** — A tree service quoting and estimate management demo
- **Bulk Material Estimator** — A customer-facing quote request flow for sand, gravel, and bulk material suppliers
- **Operations Command Center / FieldOps-style Dashboard** — An internal operations dashboard concept for tracking work, follow-ups, job status, and business activity

These demos are used to show how small businesses can replace manual, disconnected processes with simple digital tools.

## Project Purpose

This project serves as the main website for Auxilium Business Solutions and as a portfolio hub for demonstrating practical business process modernization work.

The goal is to position Auxilium as a service provider that can help businesses:

- Reduce repetitive admin work
- Improve quoting and intake workflows
- Create better operational visibility
- Standardize internal processes
- Build lightweight tools that fit real business needs
